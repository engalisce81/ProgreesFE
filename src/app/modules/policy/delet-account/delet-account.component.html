Delete Account

If you wish to delete your account and all your personal data from the The Progress Vet Academy app, you can follow these steps:

  1. Go to "Edit Profile" within the app.
  2. Select the "Delete Account" option.
  3. After confirming the deletion, all your personal data will be permanently removed from the app and cannot be recovered later.
